1. Introduction and scope

HUOC operates a website and associated self-hosted or integrated technology services under the homeguardofbill-huoc.com domain. Services may include entertainment, media, file storage, file sharing, photo libraries, document management, collaboration, identity management, technical support, remote workspaces, and related infrastructure.

This Privacy Policy explains how HUOC collects, processes, stores, protects, and discloses information when individuals access these services.

Not every service is publicly accessible, and some applications are restricted to authorized or invited users.

2. Information collected

Depending on the service, HUOC may process:

  • Technical and connection information: IP addresses, connection times, user agents, device or browser information, requested resources, errors, security events, and relevant system logs.
  • Account information: usernames, display names, email addresses, account preferences, permissions, group memberships, login history, and authentication events.
  • Files and sharing information: uploaded files, documents, filenames, sizes, timestamps, sharing permissions, recipients, links, and related collaboration metadata.
  • Media information: media library information, playback progress, playlists, viewing or listening preferences, and media requests, when recorded by the relevant service.
  • Photos and documents: user-provided images, documents, associated metadata, and content stored in permitted applications.
  • Remote sessions: connection, login, session, permission, and security-event information associated with authorized remote workspaces or remote-support services.
  • Communications and support: information submitted through emails, helpdesk tickets, booking forms, website chat, support requests, and related communications.

3. Purposes of processing

HUOC processes information to operate requested services, authenticate users, manage permissions, store and synchronize files, deliver authorized media, enable sharing, provide technical support, investigate security incidents, prevent abuse, recover systems, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Where a particular privacy law applies, the legal basis for processing depends on the activity and may include service provision, legitimate operational or security interests, legal obligations, or consent where legally required.

4. Accounts and access

Certain services require authorized accounts, invitations, or group membership.

Access is controlled through application permissions, identity systems, and other applicable security controls.

Publicly reachable login pages do not imply that public registration is permitted.

File-sharing links may be accessible to their recipients according to the configured permissions and protections.

5. Hosting and storage

HUOC operates self-managed infrastructure for some services. Data may be stored on application servers, attached storage systems, or backup systems.

Third-party providers may process limited information when they provide networking, routing, protection, authentication, scheduling, payment, or email services (see Section 8).

Self-hosting does not imply that no information is processed by third parties.

6. Retention and deletion

Retention periods depend on application configuration, operational requirements, security needs, backup schedules, and applicable law.

Files, deleted items, file versions, accounts, security records, support tickets, and backup copies may follow different retention rules. For example, file-storage applications such as Nextcloud may keep deleted items in a trash area and keep earlier versions of files for a period set by the application configuration.

Deleting content from an application does not necessarily immediately remove it from previous versions or backup copies.

HUOC will respond to applicable deletion requests subject to lawful limitations and technical backup lifecycles.

7. Cookies and analytics

Applications may use cookies, session tokens, and browser storage required for authentication, security, preferences, and application functionality.

The HUOC website

  • As of the effective date, the HUOC website source contains no analytics, advertising, or marketing-tracking scripts.
  • The website stores a single display preference (light or dark theme) in your browser’s local storage so it can remember your choice. It is not sent to HUOC.
  • The main website pages load a third-party website-chat widget provided by Chatwoot. If you use or load it, the chat provider may set its own cookies or browser storage and process your messages and technical information. See Section 8.
  • Fonts and other site assets are served by HUOC rather than a third-party content network.

These legal pages

The Privacy Policy, Legal Notice, Terms of Service, and legal hub are static pages. They use no JavaScript, set no cookies, use no browser storage, load no third-party assets, and include no advertising or analytics.

Connected applications

Sign-in and session cookies are used by login-protected applications such as Nextcloud and other HUOC-hosted services. Where a service uses analytics or nonessential tracking, or where the law requires a consent or user-choice mechanism, HUOC will implement one.

9. Security

HUOC uses safeguards appropriate to the relevant services, which may include encryption, authentication, permission restrictions, monitoring, backups, and incident response.

Security systems may process connection metadata, account identifiers, IP addresses, and relevant events.

No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Privacy rights and requests

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, export, delete, or restrict processing of their personal information, and to exercise other legally available choices.

Requests should be submitted to usreport@homeguardofbill-huoc.com.

HUOC may verify the requester’s identity or authorization before releasing or modifying account information.

Legal exceptions and other users’ rights may limit particular requests.

11. International processing

Services may be accessed from different locations. Information may be processed in the United States and, where applicable, in locations used by relevant service providers.

HUOC will assess applicable international privacy requirements according to the services and individuals concerned.

12. Children

HUOC services are not intentionally directed to children under 13. Reports involving potentially improper collection of children’s information may be submitted to usreport@homeguardofbill-huoc.com and will be addressed as required by applicable law.

13. Changes and contact

This policy may be revised when services, operational practices, or applicable laws change. The effective date will be updated when revised terms are published, and additional notice will be provided where required.
